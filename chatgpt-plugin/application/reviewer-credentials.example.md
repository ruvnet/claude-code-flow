# Reviewer access template

Provide any reviewer account only in the private OpenAI submission form. Never
commit its password, session, recovery codes, OAuth tokens, or client secret.

Confirm before submission:

- the account is dedicated to app review;
- it works from the public internet without VPN access;
- it does not require MFA, email codes, SMS codes, or a magic link;
- its permissions are limited to the workflows covered by the review cases;
- it can complete the OAuth authorization flow; and
- the credential will be rotated or disabled after review when appropriate.

Portal-only fields:

```text
Reviewer email: <enter privately>
Reviewer password: <enter privately>
Additional sign-in instructions: <enter privately>
```
